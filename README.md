## 🚀 Projects

**[CodeRed-Z](https://github.com/joshuakevin612/CodeRedV1)** · *Emergency Response Coordination Simulator*
Capacity-aware dispatch backend with a live dashboard, synthetic incident scenarios, 15-minute hospital-demand forecasts, and WebSocket updates. Backend team lead at IBM Z Datathon 2026; verified on LinuxONE with 21 tests and 32 live smoke checks.
`Python` `FastAPI` `SQLAlchemy` `PostgreSQL` `Scikit-Learn` `WebSockets` `IBM LinuxONE`

**[NeuroVascX](https://github.com/joshuakevin612/Silent-Stroke-Detection)** · *Multimodal Stroke-Risk Prediction Engine*
Combines patient vitals, ECG signals, and CT imaging into an early-warning risk score, with CNN feature extraction and an interactive Streamlit workflow.
`PyTorch` `ResNet18` `XGBoost` `Streamlit`

**[DevHub](https://github.com/joshuakevin612/Devhub)** · *Developer Analytics Platform*

`Next.js` `Tailwind CSS` `PostgreSQL` `Prisma`

`HTML5` `CSS3` `Python`

**[MEMSIM](https://github.com/joshuakevin612/Memory-management-simulator)** · *Memory Management Simulator*
